Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Spice Grill Cove ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information gathered through this website and through direct contact with our restaurant. We are a single, independently operated restaurant, and this policy applies only to information handled in connection with this site and our own communications with guests.

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, party size, and any message you write. We do not require you to create an account or provide payment information through this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Like most websites, our server and analytics tools may automatically log basic technical information, such as browser type, general location inferred from IP address, and pages visited. This information is used only in aggregate to understand how the site is used and is not linked to your identity unless you have separately provided contact details.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry, confirm a reservation, or answer a question about our restaurant. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Analytics and the Google Tag

This site uses Google's tagging service (gtag.js) to understand overall site traffic and performance, such as which pages are visited and how long visitors stay. This analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can control or opt out of this kind of tracking through your browser settings or available browser extensions.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and to maintain reasonable business records, such as reservation history. If you would like your information removed from our records, you may contact us using the details below.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, though this may limit our ability to respond to a reservation request or inquiry. You may also ask us at any time what information we hold about you or request that it be deleted, subject to any recordkeeping we are reasonably required to maintain.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or call +1-888-777-5845.
