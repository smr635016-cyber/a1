Kitchen FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an expensive dehydrator to make seed crisps?

No. All our masterclasses include adaptations for conventional home ovens set to their lowest temperature setting (170°F–200°F) with the door propped slightly ajar.

How long do homemade dehydrated herbal crackers stay crisp?

Stored in airtight glass jars with a food-safe desiccant packet, our seed crackers retain full shatter and fresh aromatic potency for 4 to 6 weeks.
