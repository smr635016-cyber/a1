1. Introduction and Scope of Policy

HerbSnackFern, operating via the domain herbsnackfern.com and situated at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data autonomy of every individual who explores our culinary platform. This Privacy Policy details our transparent protocols concerning the collection, storage, processing, and safeguarding of personal and technical data when you engage with our botanical recipes, kitchen pairing matrix, and electronic contact desks.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

We collect personal identification data only when you choose to submit it directly—such as when completing our recipe inquiry form or subscribing to our botanical kitchen updates. This information typically comprises your name, email address, and the specific text of your message. Additionally, our secure web servers automatically gather non-identifying technical analytics, including your browser software, device operating system, internet protocol address, referring URLs, and general browsing duration across our culinary masterclasses.

3. Operational Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

All information gathered by HerbSnackFern is used exclusively to maintain our website infrastructure, deliver requested recipe assistance, improve user interface responsiveness, and evaluate aggregated content popularity to plan future botanical guides. We process this data based on legitimate business interests in maintaining an informative culinary publication, as well as your direct consent when you submit inquiries to our kitchen office.

4. Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics Telemetry

Our website uses standard HTTP cookies, browser local storage tokens, and Google Analytics tracking mechanisms to evaluate visitor engagement, measure recipe reading depth, and preserve your customized interface settings (such as dark or light botanical kitchen themes). These cookies do not access confidential files or extract private data from your device. You may disable or wipe cookies through your browser settings at any time without compromising article readability.

5. Non-Monetization and Third-Party Data Sharing

We maintain an absolute standard never to sell, rent, monetize, or trade reader personal data with commercial brokers, advertising networks, or third-party marketing companies. Information is shared strictly with essential technical infrastructure partners (such as certified web hosting providers and transactional email systems) who operate under legally binding confidentiality agreements and process data solely on our behalf.

6. Data Security, Protection, and Retention

We implement comprehensive electronic, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to defend your personal information against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, loss, or destructive breach. We retain user correspondence records only for as long as necessary to resolve culinary queries or until a reader formally requests the complete deletion of their correspondence history from our secure servers.

7. Global Reader Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, HerbSnackFern upholds your fundamental rights to review, modify, export, or permanently delete any personal data maintained by our organization. Residents of the European Economic Area (EEA) and California enjoy specific rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively, including the right to opt out of data processing and to receive a transparent accounting of all collected data categories without discrimination.

8. Culinary Desk Contact Details

If you have questions, concerns, or formal data access requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data protection protocols, please contact our Data Governance Officer by mail at HerbSnackFern, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by calling our desk at +1-888-777-5845. We are committed to responding to all formal inquiries promptly and thoroughly.
