Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to help websites function properly and to provide basic information about how a site is used. This Cookie Policy explains how Spice Grill Cove uses cookies and similar technologies on this site.

Types of Cookies We Use

This website uses a limited set of cookies, primarily related to analytics. We use Google's tagging service (gtag.js) to understand overall traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and roughly how visitors found the site. These cookies help us understand how the website is performing but do not collect sensitive personal information.

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising retargeting, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies to other companies.

Why We Use These Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand which pages, such as our menu or Journal posts, are most useful to visitors, so we can keep the website organized around what people are actually looking for. This information is reviewed in aggregate rather than tied to any single visitor's identity.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking cookies entirely or clearing existing ones. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain website functions, though the core content of this site — our menu, hours, and contact information — will remain accessible either way.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the tools we use or applicable regulations. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
