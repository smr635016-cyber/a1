1. Understanding Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies are compact data packets placed onto your web browser by websites you visit. They serve essential functions such as maintaining continuous user interface customizations, providing smooth navigation between long-form culinary guides, and collecting aggregated telemetry about page loading performance. Modern browser technologies like HTML5 Local Storage are similarly utilized to preserve your preferred kitchen themes without constant server queries.

2. How HerbSnackFern Uses Cookie Technologies

HerbSnackFern uses cookies and local storage tokens strictly to enhance your browsing experience and monitor platform stability. For example, when you switch between our Light Botanical and Dark Kitchen color themes, or scale the font size of our masterclasses, a lightweight token is preserved in your local browser so your visual settings remain active throughout your visit. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies to follow your web activities across unrelated external domains.

3. Categories of Cookies Deployed on Our Site

We organize our cookies into two distinct functional categories. Strictly Necessary & Preference Cookies are vital for retaining your active theme choices, recipe font scaling, and responsive mobile navigation states; disabling these cookies may cause interface preferences to revert upon page refresh. Performance & Analytics Cookies (such as Google Analytics) aggregate anonymized metrics regarding recipe readership, scroll depth, and page speed, helping our kitchen team identify which culinary guides provide the greatest practical value.

4. Managing and Clearing Cookies via Browser Settings

You retain complete control to inspect, block, or delete cookies at any time through your internet browser's preference panel. Major browsers (including Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge) provide intuitive controls to reject third-party cookies or wipe stored cookie caches upon browser exit. Please note that while our culinary guides remain fully accessible without cookies, personalized visual themes may reset upon subsequent visits.

5. Policy Updates and Kitchen Inquiries

HerbSnackFern periodically reviews this Cookie Policy to reflect technical infrastructure improvements or relevant statutory developments. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
